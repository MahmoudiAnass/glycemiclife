How to use this database

Type in the search box to instantly filter foods by name (e.g. "banana", "rice"). Use the category dropdown to browse fruits, grains, legumes, and more. Click any column header to sort — most people find sorting by GL per serving most useful, because it ranks foods by how they'll actually affect your blood sugar in a normal portion.

Reading the badges

  • GI — Green = 55 or under (low), Amber = 56–69 (medium), Red = 70+ (high).
  • GL/serving — Green = under 10 (low), Amber = 10–19 (medium), Red = 20+ (high).

Serving sizes we used

Each row uses a realistic serving — 120 g for most fruits, 150 g for cooked rice and grains, 30 g for bread, 250 g for milk/juice — matching the reference portions used in international GI research. If you eat more or less, use the Glycemic Load Calculator to recalculate for your exact portion.
