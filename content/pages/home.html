Glycemic Load (GL) is the single most useful number in nutrition — and almost no one uses it. It tells you how much a real portion of food will actually raise your blood sugar, unlike the Glycemic Index (GI), which only rates the sugar type. GlycemicLife exists to change that: free tools, honest guides, and clear answers.
