Why look at a whole meal instead of one food?

Glycemic Load is most useful when you add it up across everything on your plate. A single food might be low-GL on its own, but pair three medium-GL foods together and the meal total can tip into "high" territory. The Meal Builder does that math for you: add each food and its portion, and watch the combined Glycemic Load update instantly.

How meal-level Glycemic Load is classified

  • Total GL under 20 — Low. A gentle, stable meal.
  • Total GL 20 to 39 — Medium. Reasonable — pay attention to portions next time.
  • Total GL 40 and up — High. Expect a bigger blood-sugar swing. Consider trimming a carb-heavy item or adding protein/fiber.
